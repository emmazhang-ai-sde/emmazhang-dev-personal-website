## Notes on the extraction

- **`CS 3--` is Algorithms.** The transcript records it only as `CS ELECTIVE`, a
  300-level CS transfer credit the university never articulated to a specific
  course, so the real title survives only on the originating institution's
  transcript, which is not in this PDF. Emma confirmed on 2026-07-24 that the
  course was Algorithms; the site lists it that way. The exact wording of the
  original title (e.g. whether it read "Algorithms", "Design and Analysis of
  Algorithms", or similar) is still unverified — the annotation is her recall,
  not a transcript quote.
- **MATH 417 vs MATH 418.** The transcript titles 417 as `Intro to Abstract
  Algebra` (no numeral) and 418 as `Intro to Abstract Algebra II`. That
  asymmetry is the transcript's, not a typo here.
- **MATH 314** is `Intro Higher Math` — no "to", and "Math" not "Mathematics".
- **MATH 402** is `Non Euclidean Geometry` — no hyphen in "Non Euclidean".
- **MATH 441** appears in the PDF with an `ﬀ` ligature (`Diﬀerential`); written
  here with ordinary letters, which is the same text, not a change of wording.
- **STAT 400** is listed separately because its prefix is neither MATH nor CS,
  though it sits in the maths sequence. The site currently groups it with maths.
- Untitled non-CS/non-maths transfer credits (`ACCY 2--`, `PHIL 1--`, and the
  `XFER 1--` entries) are excluded — none is a CS or maths course, but note that
  a course failing to articulate loses its title, so this PDF cannot rule out
  that one of them was quantitative.
